Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Welcome to myLB (My Learning Buddy). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when using the myLB mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be processed by third-party services, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, information generated through your use of myLB, and certain technical information collected automatically.

Account Information

When you create or maintain an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account credentials, including your password or authentication information
  • Other information you voluntarily provide as part of your account

Study Materials and User-Generated Content

myLB allows you to create and use educational content. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Notes and text you enter into the app
  • Study materials you upload
  • Flashcards and study sets you create
  • Mind maps and information contained within your Infinite Canvas
  • Text or voice responses submitted during Feynman Mode
  • Questions or problems submitted to AI Solve
  • Information used to generate AI-powered study plans

This information is used to provide the learning features you request.

Learning and Gamification Data

When you use myLB, we may collect information about your learning activity, including:

  • Study progress
  • Study sessions and interaction history
  • Feynman Mode activity
  • Study set and flashcard progress
  • Study plan progress
  • Focus Pet status and progression
  • Pet XP, evolution, and related game progress
  • Achievements and milestone badges
  • Trophy Room information
  • Co-op Quest participation and progress
  • Group challenge contributions and Group Relics
  • Learning analytics and performance information

Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information when you use the application, such as:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version
  • General technical information about your device
  • Information about how you interact with the application
  • Error and diagnostic information

We use this information primarily to maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve myLB.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your myLB account.
  • Provide and maintain the core features of the application.
  • Convert your notes and study materials into study sets and flashcards.
  • Generate and display mind maps through the Infinite Canvas.
  • Provide Feynman Mode and evaluate your explanations to provide AI-powered feedback.
  • Generate personalized study plans based on the information you provide.
  • Provide AI Solve and other educational assistance features.
  • Track your learning progress and study activity.
  • Maintain your Focus Pet, XP, evolution, achievements, and other gamification features.
  • Provide co-op quests, group challenges, shared progress, and Group Relics.
  • Display learning analytics and progress information.
  • Improve the functionality, reliability, security, and user experience of myLB.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical problems, abuse, fraud, or security issues.
  • Respond to questions, support requests, and other communications.
  • Communicate important information about the application, including updates, security notices, and changes to our services or policies.

3. Artificial Intelligence and Third-Party Processing

myLB uses artificial intelligence to provide features such as Feynman Mode, study-set generation, mind maps, study planning, AI Solve, and other AI-powered learning functionality.

DeepSeek

At this time, myLB uses DeepSeek as its third-party AI service provider.

Depending on the feature you use, information you submit to myLB may be sent to DeepSeek for processing so that the requested AI functionality can be provided.

For example, this may include:

  • Text from your study materials
  • Questions or prompts you submit
  • Your explanations during Feynman Mode
  • Transcribed voice input, where applicable
  • Other content necessary to generate the AI response you requested

We do not use your study content for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the functionality of myLB unless otherwise explained in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information to third-party AI providers.

You should avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information into study materials, prompts, or other content unless it is necessary for your use of the application.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party service providers to help us operate myLB, such as providers involved in:

  • AI processing
  • Cloud infrastructure and data storage
  • Authentication
  • Analytics
  • Application security
  • Technical support

These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to provide their services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or government request.
  • Protect the rights, safety, or property of myLB, our users, or others.
  • Detect, prevent, or investigate fraud, abuse, security issues, or other unlawful activity.

Business Transfers

If myLB is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, information associated with the application may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable privacy laws.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically is completely secure. Therefore, while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide myLB's services, maintain your account, provide requested functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our legitimate interests.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete it or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply to you, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account or personal information.
  • Request restrictions on certain processing of your information.
  • Object to certain uses of your information.
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any third-party service you interact with.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that we do not control.

12. International Data Processing

Depending on where you live and where our service providers operate, your information may be processed or stored in countries other than your country of residence.

Where required by applicable law, we will take appropriate measures to provide protection for your information when it is transferred internationally.
